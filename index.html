Welcome home

A home where the broken become whole.

Founded on 9 March 2009 by Apostle Dr. Sunday Sinyangwe and Pastor Bupe Lumbwa Sinyangwe after seventeen years of faithful service in Pentecostal ministry, Shalom Embassy Ministries International began in Lusaka's Kanyama area and now serves from our headquarters at Plot 995, Garden House, Off Mumbwa Road, with over 21 branches across Zambia.

Together with The Standing In The Gap Team Prayer Ministry and Virtuous Women Ministry, we are commissioned to preach the full Gospel of Jesus Christ, take deliverance to the captives, and equip believers to fulfil their destinies in the Lord.

  • Sound biblical teaching & discipleship
  • Standing In The Gap national intercession
  • 21+ branches across Lusaka & Zambia
  • Divine healing, deliverance & restoration
