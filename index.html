Welcome home

A home where the broken become whole.

Founded on 9 March 2009 by Apostle Dr. Sunday Sinyangwe and Pastor Bupe Lumbwa Sinyangwe, Shalom Embassy Ministries International began in Lusaka's Kanyama area and now serves from our headquarters at Plot 995, Garden House, Off Mumbwa Road, with over 21 branches across Zambia.

Together with The Standing In The Gap Team Prayer Ministry and Virtuous Women Ministry, we are commissioned to preach the full Gospel of Jesus Christ, take deliverance to the captives, and equip believers to fulfil their destinies in the Lord.

  • Sound biblical teaching & discipleship
  • Standing In The Gap national intercession
  • 21+ branches across Lusaka & Zambia
  • Divine healing, deliverance & restoration

Questions people ask

Before you come.

A few honest answers for first-time guests and friends.

What should I expect on Sunday?

A warm welcome, heartfelt worship, prayer and a practical message from the Word. The service usually lasts several hours. You are free to participate as much as you are comfortable.

Where do you meet?

We meet at Plot 995, Garden House, Off Mumbwa Road, Behind Livonia Shopping Complex, Lusaka. Our monthly 7 Hours service begins at 07:00 AM on the last Sunday.

What should I wear?

Come as you are. Some people dress up, others come in everyday clothes. What matters is that you are here.

Is there something for children and teenagers?

Yes. Our Battle Axe Youth Ministry and children’s activities make room for the next generation to learn, ask questions and belong.

How do I get involved?

Start by joining a Sunday service or a midweek gathering. Then talk to us about Standing In The Gap, Virtuous Women, Battle Axe Youth or serving on a team. We will help you find your place.

Can I watch online?

Yes. You can follow the ministry on YouTube, Facebook and Instagram, or join our video conference from your phone, tablet or computer.
