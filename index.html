Welcome home

A home where the broken become whole.

For over seventeen years, Shalom Embassy Ministries International has been a place of healing, deliverance, and restoration in Lusaka, Zambia. What began as a prayer burden has grown into a family of believers hungry for the presence of God.

We are led by Apostle Sunday Sinyangwe, a teacher of the Word and a man of prayer, together with Pastor Bube Sinyangwe. Our mandate is simple: that every person would know the peace of God and leave whole — nothing missing, nothing broken.

  • A church built on the Word
  • A house of prayer for all nations
  • A home for the whole family
  • A place of healing and deliverance
