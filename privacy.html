Privacy

Your privacy matters to us.

How Shalom Embassy Ministries International handles personal information shared through this website.

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

Shalom Embassy Ministries International is a church in Lusaka, Zambia. Our international headquarters is at Plot 995, Garden House, Off Mumbwa Road, Behind Livonia Shopping Complex, Lusaka, Zambia. You can reach us on +260 974 807 770 or +260 977 846 660, or by WhatsApp at +260 974 807 770.

What we collect

When you use a form on this website, you may choose to share your name, phone number, email address, prayer request, testimony, visit details or any other information you include in your message.

Our hosting provider may also process basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type and pages visited, in order to serve the site securely.

How we use it

  • To respond to your prayer request, enquiry or message.
  • To help you plan a visit, arrange transport or connect with a ministry.
  • To follow up on a testimony or connect card, with your consent.
  • To keep the website secure and understand how it is used.

Forms and messaging

Some forms on this site open your own messaging or email application so that you can send a message directly to the church. In that case, your information is handled by your provider and by the church's messaging or email service.

Where a form sends information to the church's booking or prayer service, it is used only to respond to your request and is not added to a marketing list.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with trusted service providers who help us operate the website, messaging or video meetings, or where we are required to do so by law.

Cookies and analytics

This website may use basic cookies or similar technologies to remember your preferences and keep the site working. We do not use them to track you across other websites.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or withdraw consent for future contact. Contact the church office on +260 974 807 770 or by WhatsApp and we will respond as soon as we can.

Children

We care about the privacy of children. Please do not submit personal information about a child through this website without the consent of a parent or guardian. Children's ministry records are handled separately by the church office.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information shared with us. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so please avoid sending sensitive information unless it is necessary.

Changes

We may update this notice from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Contact Shalom Embassy Ministries International at Plot 995, Garden House, Off Mumbwa Road, Behind Livonia Shopping Complex, Lusaka, Zambia, or call +260 974 807 770 / +260 977 846 660.
