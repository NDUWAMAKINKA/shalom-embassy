Good to know

Your questions, answered.

Practical information to help you plan your visit to Shalom Embassy Headquarters.

Where exactly is the church located?

Our international headquarters is located at Plot 995, Garden House, Off Mumbwa Road, Behind Livonia Shopping Complex (Behind Garden House Hotel), Lusaka, Zambia. We also have over 21 branches across Lusaka (including Mtendere, Mandevu, Matero, Foxdale, and Kanyama) and Chibombo.

What are the Sunday and midweek service times?

On Sundays, we gather for the 7 Hours of Nothing Missing, Nothing Broken healing and deliverance service on the last Sunday of every month (07:00 to 14:00 CAT), Big Sunday quarterly, and Empowerment Sunday on the first Sunday of every month (07:00 to 14:00 CAT). During the week, Morning Glory holds Monday to Saturday at 06:00 CAT, Virtuous Women meets Tuesdays at 14:00 CAT, Midweek Service holds Wednesdays at 11:00 and 16:00 CAT, Night of Solution holds Wednesday nights from 21:00 to 04:00 CAT, and Healing & Deliverance holds Thursdays at 18:00 CAT. My Worship My Weapon 4 holds on the first Friday of every month at 21:00 CAT.

Where do I park and who can help me on arrival?

Parking is available within and around the church premises at Plot 995, Garden House. Our ushers and protocol team will direct you as soon as you arrive. For directions, call +260 977 846 660 or +260 974 807 770.

Are children and youths catered for?

Yes. Our Children's Ministry and Battle Axe Youth Ministry provide dedicated teaching, discipleship, and fellowship for children, teenagers, and university students.

How can I order the Jubilee Prayer Book or Everyday With The Holy Spirit?

Published books and prayer guides by Apostle Dr. Sunday Sinyangwe and Pastor Bupe Sinyangwe are available in print at the church bookshop in Lusaka, via WhatsApp on +260 974 807 770, and as digital downloads on Selar and Amazon Kindle. See the Books section for details.

Can I watch services live online?

Yes. Our gatherings are streamed live on Shalom TV on YouTube and Facebook. You can also watch replays anytime in our sermon library.
